# Jobs to be done

Each job is something the person needs to do whether or not this calculator
exists. No job names the app, a screen or a control.

## J-1 — Know the monthly cost of a given loan

Role: R-1

When I am working out whether I can afford a particular property, I want to
know what a mortgage of a given size would cost me each month, so I can tell
whether it fits in my monthly budget before I commit to anything.

## J-2 — Judge what a change in rate is worth

Role: R-1

When a lender quotes me a rate, I want to know what a percentage point either
way does to my monthly payment, so I can decide whether shopping around or
waiting is worth the effort.

## J-3 — Trade the monthly cost against the length of the loan

Role: R-1

When I am choosing between a shorter and a longer term, I want to know what
the term does to the monthly payment, so I can set the monthly cost against
being free of the debt sooner.

## J-4 — Know what an interest-only deal really commits me to

Role: R-2

When I am offered an interest-only mortgage, I want to know both what I pay
each month and what I will still owe when the term ends, so I can judge
whether I have a credible way of repaying the capital.

## J-5 — Compare an interest-only quote against a repayment quote honestly

Role: R-2

When I am weighing an interest-only quote against a repayment quote, I want
to understand what the lower monthly figure is leaving out, so I can avoid
choosing it for the wrong reason.

## J-6 — Check figures I have been given

Role: R-1, R-2

When a broker or a lender hands me figures, I want to work them out myself,
so I can tell whether what I have been told is right.

## J-7 — Be sure I have not got a figure wrong

Role: R-1, R-2

When I am working from a quote in front of me, I want to be certain I have
not transcribed one of the numbers wrongly, so I can avoid acting on an
answer that was never right in the first place.

## J-8 — Agree a schedule on an interest-free loan

Role: R-1

When money is being lent to me at no interest, by family for instance, I want
to know what the monthly repayment would be, so I can agree a schedule
everyone can hold to.
